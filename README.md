# Kunpeng Xie · 谢鲲鹏

**Multimodal learning, representation alignment, and controllable generation.**

[Website](https://kunpeng.blog/en) · [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=gzf1ng4AAAAJ) · [Email](mailto:12310102@mail.sustech.edu.cn)

I'm a final-year undergraduate in Intelligent Medical Engineering at **SUSTech**, working with Prof. Quanying Liu at the **Neural Computing and Control Lab**.

I study what representations preserve—and whether that information is useful for understanding, generation, and interaction. My work began with neural signals and is expanding toward general multimodal models and feedback-driven generative systems.

## Research

**[MindPilot](https://openreview.net/forum?id=7jdmXx869Q) — ICLR 2026, co-first author**

EEG-guided, closed-loop visual generation. I developed the controllable diffusion module and co-designed the feedback experiments. [Code ↗](https://github.com/ncclab-sustech/MindPilot)

**Brain Hierarchical Alignment — work in progress**

Can EEG carry enough information to reconstruct dense visual patch tokens? I investigate how representation granularity affects retrieval and generation. The manuscript is being revised for resubmission to ICLR 2027.
